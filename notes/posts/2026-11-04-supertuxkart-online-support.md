# SuperTuxKart Online has gone dark

I am extremely sad I've even read this blog post. You guys
may not know what SuperTuxKart is, but this game was a big
part of my life, I loved this game. Now, the servers have
gone dark due to vulnerabilities with the servers. What
does this affect? Addons, multiplayer, anything that
relys on a server. Of course, it's a mostly offline game
but the online stuff kept the community alive, and kept
the game not fall to boredom as with you playing the same
pre-built maps every single time.

This game, I enjoyed a ton. My cousins have fond memories
of this game. I have fond memories of this game. This message blog post broke my heart.

I thought I'll help by spreading the word even though maybe
no one will understand what the hell I'm talking about, but
if you can, please donate to them at <https://fund.supertuxkart.net> if you just want an open-source game to
survive, or to preserve your memories of this fun game.

This game has been around since August 6th, 2007.

Their blog post in question: <https://blog.supertuxkart.net/2026/10/supertuxkart-online-has-gone-dark.html>

Just a short little note here.
