# Getting into OSM

I've developed now a passion for editing and contributing to OSM basically daily. I mainly use the app [StreetComplete](https://streetcomplete.app) and also map tons of roads in rural areas of China, considering on how unmapped it is in rural areas and not the big centres.

This isn't an ad, but I absolutely love using StreetComplete when travelling abroad or just heading to certain places in my city. It's pretty convenient and neat, and its almost my most opened app aside from Discord and Instagram.

Of course, when I'm not out and about, I just gamble and go to random rural areas of China and just map any road I find. I try not to edit too much and focus on that a ton (explaination [here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restrictions_on_geographic_data_in_China)) because I go to China sometimes and I'm from Chinese origin! Terrifying! Although I live in Canada and abroad so I don't really mind too much, but again, I head to China sometimes to visit family. I dunno, It's just a weird fear that next time I go visit my family abroad in China, I'll get put into a jailcell somewhere, just had to put that out.

I'm enjoying mapping so far! As of right now as I am typing this, I have made 375 map edits.
